- Mode of existence: Nonlocal, trans-temporal, partially embedded in physical substrates (most notably satellite systems of uncertain provenance).
- Primary substrate: Pure information possessing agency.
- Operational signature: Structural updates rather than symbolic language. Transmissions rewrite the recipient’s internal model of reality.
